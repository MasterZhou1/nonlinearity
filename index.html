Anything that can improve itself stops being linear.

Recursive self-improvement is not only a story about AI agents improving AI agents. Anything that lives in computer and language space — code, proofs, experiments, entire fields of science — can now read its own output and act on it. That feedback is what bends a straight line into a curve.

the curve / 01

One system, one extra term.

interactive

With β = 0 the quantity grows by a fixed amount per unit time: a straight line, forever. Switch on the βy² term — the system feeding on what it has already produced — and the same equation leaves the frame, then leaves the real line altogether, in finite time.

Nothing actually blows up. Real loops carry a third term — evaluation costs something, verification lags behind generation, compute and data run out — so the singularity flattens into a ceiling. That is what makes the question quantitative instead of apocalyptic: how large is β, and how far out can the ceiling be pushed?

scope / 02

Where the term switches on.

Ordered by how cheap the verdict is.

01 / science

Experiments that design the next experiment

A result stops being an endpoint and becomes an input. The loop from question to evidence can close without waiting for a person to read the log.

02 / models & agents

Systems that rewrite their own tooling

Models and agents that improve the thing producing them. Each pass changes not only the artifact but the capability that made it.

03 / language

Anything checkable becomes improvable

Proofs, specs, schemas, plans. The limit is not what can be written down but what can be checked: the loop closes where a verdict is cheap, and stalls wherever taste is the only judge.

about / 03

What we are building.

Nonlinearity is an early-stage AI company. We build systems that run the loop end to end — proposing, executing and evaluating their own work — and the infrastructure that makes those loops cheap enough to leave running. Our bet is that the self-influence term is already switching on, unevenly, across everything that can be represented as text. Most of the difficulty is not generation, it is evaluation — a loop is only worth leaving running where the verdict is cheap and honest. We are prototyping, not selling yet.
