The loop

01 / PROPOSE

Hypotheses with a prior

Candidate experiments are drafted from what previous runs actually showed, not from a fresh prompt each time.

02 / RUN

Experiments as infrastructure

Jobs are dispatched, checkpointed and monitored on your own compute, with every config and seed recorded.

03 / TRIAGE

Kill branches early

Results are scored against the hypothesis, negative results are kept, and dead directions are pruned automatically.

Why now

The cost of running one more experiment keeps falling, while the number of plausible experiments keeps growing. The bottleneck has moved: the hard part is no longer running a study, it is choosing which study is worth running and knowing when to stop. That selection step is mechanical enough to automate, and consequential enough to be worth automating well.

About

Nonlinearity is an early-stage AI company. We work on systems that run the experimental loop end to end — proposing, executing and evaluating their own experiments — and on the infrastructure that makes those loops cheap enough to leave running. We are prototyping, not selling yet.
